Changelog

What's new

Every Storm release, newest first. Storm updates itself, so you're always on the latest.

1.1.0 November 18, 2026 Latest

A big one, with lots of things people asked for.

New

  • A proper installer.
  • Password manager.
  • Tab groups and workspaces.
  • Split view.
  • Reader mode and picture-in-picture.
  • Download manager.
  • Crash recovery.
  • More AI tools in the Storm AI sidebar.
  • Extensions and shareable themes.

1.0.0 November 18, 2026 First release

The first public release of Storm.

Browsing

  • Tabs, bookmarks and history, built on Microsoft Edge WebView2.
  • Stay signed in to Google, Discord and other sites.
  • Import from Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave and Opera.
  • 20 search engines with address-bar keyword shortcuts (for example b cats).
  • Command palette with Ctrl + K.

Look and feel

  • Lightning-strike startup intro with procedural thunder, fully customizable.
  • Video and image wallpapers, custom cursors.
  • Custom layouts: vertical tabs, bottom toolbar, saved presets.
  • Built-in music player.
  • Around 100 settings.

Focus and AI

  • Study mode: Pomodoro timer, distracting-site blocking, AI quizzes and flashcards.
  • Storm AI sidebar: free local AI on your GPU, or Claude with your own API key.

Privacy and safety

  • Ad and tracker blocking.
  • Local data encrypted with Windows DPAPI. No telemetry.
  • Content filters, lock settings and PIN-protected parental controls.
  • Signed auto-updates.
