Do I need an add-on to use Storm?

No. Storm works fully without any add-on. Ad blocking, the AI sidebar, study mode and everything else are free.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime. Checkout and billing are handled by Stripe.

Does Rainshield or Mirage see what I browse?

Rainshield checks downloads with your PC's own antivirus engine and checks sites against lists downloaded to your PC, so nothing about your browsing is sent anywhere. Mirage routes your traffic through Storm's servers only while you have it turned on. The privacy page has the details.

Can I use one subscription on more than one PC?

Yes, on up to 5 PCs.
