Privacy

Your data stays on your PC

Plain-language answers about what Storm stores and where anything goes.

The short version: Storm has no telemetry and no account. Your browsing data is stored locally and encrypted. The only time page content leaves your PC for an AI service is if you add a Claude API key and use it.

No telemetry

Storm does not send usage statistics, crash analytics, browsing history or identifiers to us or anyone else. We don't run a server that collects information about you.

What's stored, and how

History, bookmarks, settings, saved layouts and similar data are kept on your device only. Storm encrypts this data with Windows DPAPI (Data Protection API), which ties the encryption to your Windows user account, another user on the same PC can't read it.

Cookies and site logins (so you stay signed in to Google, Discord and others) are stored locally by Microsoft Edge WebView2, the engine Storm uses to display pages.

You can clear your data at any time from Storm's settings, or by uninstalling Storm and deleting its data folder.

Storm AI

  • Local AI (default free option): runs an open model on your own GPU. Your prompts and the page text it reads are processed on your PC and are not sent anywhere.
  • Claude (optional): only works if you add your own Anthropic API key. When you use it, your prompt and the text of the page you're asking about are sent to Anthropic to generate a response. That data is handled under Anthropic's own privacy policy and terms. Your key is stored locally, encrypted with DPAPI.
  • If you never add a key, nothing is ever sent to Anthropic.

Secure tabs (Tor)

Secure tabs route traffic through the Tor network. Sites you visit see a Tor exit node's address instead of yours, and your internet provider sees that you're using Tor but not which sites you visit. Tor relays are run by volunteers, not by us. Secure tabs add anonymity but are not a substitute for Tor Browser's full hardening against serious threats.

Ad & tracker blocking

Blocking happens on your device. Storm doesn't report which requests it blocked or which sites you visited.

Network requests Storm itself makes

  • Update checks: Storm downloads a small update manifest from this website to see if a new version exists. Like any web request, the host sees your IP address. No identifiers are attached. Updates are signature-checked before install.
  • Search: searches go to the search engine you choose (Storm supports 20). That engine's privacy policy applies.
  • Websites you visit: work exactly as in any browser, those sites see your requests.

Parental controls

Content filters and lock settings, including the PIN, are stored locally and encrypted. They are never sent anywhere.

This website

This site is static. It sets no cookies and has no analytics or third-party scripts. Your hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page) as part of serving the site.

Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be listed in the changelog and this page will show the new date.
