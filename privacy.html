Privacy

Your data stays on your PC

Plain answers about what Storm keeps, and the few times anything leaves your computer.

The short version: Storm has no telemetry and no account. Your browsing data lives on your PC, encrypted. Something only leaves your PC when you turn on a feature that needs it, like Claude in the AI sidebar or the Mirage VPN.

No telemetry

Storm doesn't send usage stats, crash analytics, browsing history or tracking IDs to us or anyone else.

What's stored, and how

History, bookmarks, saved passwords, settings and layouts are kept on your PC only. Storm encrypts them with Windows DPAPI, which ties the encryption to your Windows account, so another user on the same PC can't read them.

Cookies and site logins (so you stay signed in to Google, Discord and others) are stored on your PC by Microsoft Edge WebView2, the engine Storm uses to show web pages.

You can clear your data from Storm's settings at any time, or uninstall Storm and delete its data folder.

Storm AI

  • Local AI (free): runs an open model on your own graphics card. Your questions and the page it reads stay on your PC.
  • Claude (optional): only works if you add your own Anthropic API key. When you use it, your question and the text of the page you're asking about are sent to Anthropic, and their privacy policy applies. Your key is stored on your PC, encrypted.
  • If you never add a key, nothing is ever sent to Anthropic.

Paid add-ons

  • Rainshield checks downloads with your PC's own antivirus engine, and checks sites against block lists that are downloaded to your PC. Nothing about your browsing is sent anywhere.
  • Mirage routes your traffic through Storm's servers while it's turned on, so websites see Storm's address instead of yours. When Mirage is off, your traffic goes directly to sites as usual.
  • Leaked password check (RainProtection) never sends your password. It sends only the first 5 characters of a SHA-1 hash of it, gets back a list of matching leaked hashes, and compares them on your PC.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. We don't see or store your card details.

Ad and tracker blocking

Blocking happens on your PC. Storm doesn't report what it blocked or which sites you visited.

Requests Storm makes on its own

  • Update checks: Storm downloads a small update file from this website to see if there's a new version. Like any web request, the server sees your IP address. No IDs are attached, and each update's signature is checked before it installs.
  • Search: your searches go to the search engine you choose, and that engine's privacy policy applies.

Parental controls

Content filters, lock settings and the PIN are stored on your PC, encrypted, and never sent anywhere.

This website

This site sets no cookies and has no analytics. Its font is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for it. The host may keep standard server logs, like IP address and the page requested, as part of serving pages.

Changes

If this page changes, we'll note it in the changelog.
